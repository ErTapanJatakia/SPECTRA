# SPECTRA Record Publication & Editorial Governance

## Publication workflow

`draft → research-review → safety-review → rights-review → approved → published`

Additional states are `stale`, `withdrawn` and `rejected`.

A record must not be marked published until its identity, core facts, source links, content-safety review and rights treatment are complete.

## Confidence classification

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified | The specific claim is supported by a suitable primary source. |
| Corroborated | Multiple credible sources support the claim. |
| Estimated | The claim is approximate or incompletely documented and is explicitly labelled. |
| Disputed | Credible sources conflict or a material factual disagreement remains unresolved. |

Confidence is not the same as publication status. A record can be supported by a primary source but still await safety or rights review.

Published records with record-level disputed confidence are blocked by the validator. Individual disputed claims should be omitted or clearly qualified until resolved.

## Source hierarchy

1. Official government, armed-force, ministry and intergovernmental sources.
2. Original manufacturer/developer publications and relevant standards bodies.
3. Academic or peer-reviewed research.
4. Reputable supporting publications.

A source's authority is claim-specific. Manufacturer material may be primary for product designation but is not automatically independent evidence for comparative performance claims.

## Rights and attribution

- Link to external sources by default; do not copy source text, diagrams, photographs or logos without a documented right to do so.
- Store a clear asset/content reuse status.
- Treat third-party media licences separately from the SPECTRA proprietary repository licence.
- Keep attribution and licence notices with each asset.
- If reuse rights are undetermined, do not republish the asset.

## Safety boundary

Approved educational content may cover public identification, historical development, broad platform classifications, institutional context, non-operational characteristics and general safety/legal awareness.

Exclude operational weapon-use procedures, procedural firearms assembly/disassembly, targeting or firing calculations, tactical attack guidance, weapon modification, ammunition construction, operational exploitation of vulnerabilities and offensive cyber instructions.

The fact that a detail is publicly accessible does not make it suitable for SPECTRA publication.

## Verification and maintenance

Every published equipment record carries a last-verification date and source IDs. Recheck records when the underlying source changes, a platform status is materially updated, an operator relationship changes or a source URL becomes unavailable. Mark records stale or withdraw them when they can no longer be responsibly represented.

## Editorial independence

SPECTRA is an independent supplementary educational initiative. It does not represent official military doctrine, government policy, UN curricula, manufacturer endorsement or institutional certification.
